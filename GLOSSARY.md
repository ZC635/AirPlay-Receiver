# AirPlay Receiver

Domain language for mirroring reception and explicitly enabled troubleshooting.

## Language

**Diagnostic Session**:
A troubleshooting record explicitly enabled for one AirPlay Receiver process. It is distinct from an iPhone mirroring session.
_Avoid_: Mirroring session (a different concept)

**Diagnostic Lifecycle**:
The progression of a Diagnostic Session from activation through optional handoff to a diagnostic child process, collection, and termination. Its duration is independent of a mirroring session.
_Avoid_: Application startup (a broader concept)
