# AirPlay Receiver

Terms describing receiver discovery and mirrored-video playback.

## Language

**Receiver Discovery**: The advertised receiver identity and its availability to AirPlay senders, including recovery after a name or video-quality change.

**Video Lifecycle**: The lifetime of mirrored-video playback resources, including initialization, reuse across connections, reset, and final shutdown.

**Video Reset**: Recreation of video playback resources while the receiver remains running. An ordinary disconnect and reconnect reuse those resources instead.
